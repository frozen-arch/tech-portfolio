# Hi, I'm Ogooluwa 👋

Thanks for taking the time to check out my portfolio.

I'm a self taught developer and Mechatronics Engineering student from Nigeria. I enjoy building products from idea to deployment. Most of my experience is in web development, backend systems, blockchain applications, and automation tools. Outside of programming, I also enjoy UI/UX design, graphic design, branding, and video editing because I like being involved in the entire process of bringing ideas to life.

This repository marks the beginning of me rebuilding my portfolio.

Late last year, I lost access to years of work after a security breach that also resulted in a significant financial loss. Shortly afterwards, my laptop and phone were stolen. Along with them, I lost my source code, project files, development environment, contacts, and the portfolio I had built over the years.

Looking back, I also realized I made a mistake. I got into tech at a young age, and because things moved so quickly in the crypto space, I spent more time building than documenting my work. I never imagined I could lose everything overnight, so I didn't maintain the portfolio and backups I should have. It was a difficult lesson, but one that completely changed the way I approach my work.

I'm currently rebuilding everything from scratch, and this repository is where I'll be documenting that journey. Every project I complete moving forward will be added here as I continue learning, improving, and creating.

Thanks for taking the time to visit.
